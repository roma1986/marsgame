# References and credits

Everything below was used in **Mission Architect** (the game «Архітектор місії»). Items are grouped by what they are used for. Where an external resource is open or freely available, it is listed anyway.

## 1. NASA data and resources

| Resource | How we used it |
|---|---|
| [NASA NSSDC Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/) | Distances from the Sun, orbital periods, surface temperature and pressure of Mars, Venus and Jupiter. They drive the solar-power and communication models and the launch windows. |
| NASA mission and instrument pages (for example [Mars 2020 Perseverance](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/)) and NASA articles (for example the [MOXIE results](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/perseverance-rover/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/)) | Real masses, power, instruments and results of Perseverance, Curiosity, InSight, MRO, LRO, OSIRIS-REx, Europa Clipper, Magellan and others. Every component in the game has a real prototype; the numbers and what was simplified are shown in the game («Детальніше» window and «Про дані»). |
| [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov) | All photographs in the game (section 2 below). |

## 4. Concepts, formulas and facts

- Tsiolkovsky rocket equation (propellant mass for a velocity change).
- Inverse-square law (sunlight and radio signal strength with distance).
- Hohmann-type launch windows from synodic periods of the planets (simplified circular orbits).
- Laplace's rule of succession for launch-vehicle reliability, using real launch histories.
- NASA project risk classes A–D and Discovery / New Frontiers / Flagship mission classes (our budgets and multipliers are game values).
- The "This really happened" cards retell public events of real missions (for example Mars Climate Orbiter, Opportunity, Spirit, Galileo, Beagle 2). Some of them have not been checked in detail yet.
